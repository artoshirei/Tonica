# Tonica 1.2.0

Tonica is now something you can play, not only read.

* Click any chord in the key to hear it. Press 1 to 7 to do the same from the keyboard.
* Each suggested progression has a play button, so you can hear I – V – vi – IV before you copy it.
* Keyboard control: Left and Right move by fourths and fifths, Up and Down switch between major and relative minor, Space replays the chord, Return plays the scale.
* Notes ring into each other like a real keyboard. A new chord or phrase fades the previous one out.
* The circle responds when you point at a key.
* Copy the scale, next to Play scale.
* Guitar fret numbers 3, 5, 7, 9 and 12 stand out, the way inlays guide you on a real neck.
* Scale degrees sit directly under their notes in every key.
* Tonica remembers whether the instrument shows the scale or the chord.

Fixes

* The shortcut and the menu bar icon bring Tonica forward when it is behind other windows or on another Space. Before, they hid it.
* The right-click menu says Show or Hide according to what will actually happen, including when Tonica is minimized or hidden.
* Text labels no longer show a text cursor or steal keyboard focus when clicked.
* Copy confirmation is a checkmark on the button. Buttons keep their labels and their size.
* C major and A minor read "No sharps or flats".
